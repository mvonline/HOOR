# Narrative framework review — design package 5.7

Date: 2026-11-02. Authorized scope: the user's request to recheck logic, suspense, dynamic play, fair surprises and motivation to finish. This is an editorial review and revision by the same agent, not an independent review, market evaluation or player study.

## Assessment

The strongest existing premise is learning which sounds deserve a response, then learning when to stop an unnecessary action. Ordinary Iranian work, companionship and a physically persistent vehicle make that premise specific. The revised structure uses those elements as playable evidence. It does not add a hidden mastermind, an all-explaining experiment, a dream reveal, random rule changes or a lethal-sacrifice reward.

The largest structural weaknesses were a long compulsory opening, repeated explanatory scenes, missing on-scene delivery of promised recordings, early disclosure of production-only information, an unexplained pre-finale wait, and an extended human confrontation after the supernatural climax. These are textually addressed below. The twenty-year survival supply chain remains a substantive unresolved problem; the design is not ready for a final narrative lock.

## Changes in the owning documents

1. **Opening and duration.** Chapter 0 targets 30 core minutes instead of 55. It retains one useful recovery tutorial, key relationship beats, the water clock, the incomplete answer and the relief call. Additional inspection, a second repair, the Sirch memory and side branches are optional. Chapter 10 targets about 10 playable minutes rather than 25, separating a short preparation from an explicit, player-confirmed story-time transition. The real 4:45 countdown and ten-second dawn window remain intact. Core design target: **260 minutes**, plus optional content and epilogue. This is a re-editing target, not measured playtime.
2. **A playable information ladder.** A chapter map records the current task, active question, observable change and reason to continue. The car's weld and Bahram's notebook establish repeated entry before the UI may name the seventh entry. Comparing corresponding report lines precedes the confession. Author-only identifiers are not automatic subtitles or objective spoilers. Important action information is never withheld to manufacture a twist.
3. **Distinct interactions.** Source localization, correcting a false interpretation, choosing a recovery method, opening the frame, blocking contact, reading Dara's movement, comparing evidence, selecting structural supports and changing the rescue rope path have different purposes. Five wedges cannot be implemented as five copies of one button press. Companions have tasks and limited knowledge.
4. **Missing content and continuity.** Tape 2 has a location in the geophone bag; tape 4 is accessible before optional H05; tape 5 is a timestamped stored field recording. Promised E03–E06 playbacks now occur in their named chapters with separate physical sources. Declining a recording cannot lock essential survival information or leave Kian's status dependent on a completionist run.
5. **F01–F06.** The water yard precedes the garage cut; chapter 3 returns on foot to its single parking area; chapter 6's vehicle/winch sequence is linear; chapter 5's sleeve scratch is conditional; the chapter 9 hand cut has an explicit shared cause; the pre-finale time transition requires explicit confirmation rather than dialogue skipping. These are editorial fixes awaiting executable verification.
6. **Responsibility and knowledge.** Daniyal admits withholding the unconsciousness information; he does not suddenly claim authorship of Rostam's dispatch summary. Sara learns of the covered service-map branch when Daniyal shows her the paper. Yunus's tracker denial occurs only after a question about discovered evidence.
7. **Ending coherence.** Each route represents an execution preference, not moral rank. The safe circle remains a valid complete choice. No necessary rescue or evidence is withheld to force the dangerous alternatives. Headset radio and the locator do not cross an otherwise disconnected boundary; the last position is identified as stale. Sara's unsuccessful outside call is not proof of communication into the loop.
8. **Hambar's resolution.** H07 is a short denouement, with optional longer discussion. Its coercion grows out of transport receipts, rental claims and selective reporting. The crew has no new knowledge of Hoor. The independent aid route is visible, and receiving first aid does not mean surrendering evidence or accepting an incomplete receipt.

Original expansion inputs and earlier review results remain identifiable as historical records. README and master prompt reference 5.7. The older approved production plan now has an explicit impact notice; this editorial request is not described as approval of the expanded campaign's cost, schedule or production tasks. The playtest roadmap now identifies native Unity PC as the current test target and retains earlier web descriptions as history.

## Remaining blocker: F07

The current script gives Bahram 7,305 personal days, while mentioning storage, recycling, cultivation and an independent generator without a sufficient material and maintenance account. Correct time arithmetic does not solve that causal problem. This review neither introduces infinite resources nor silently changes his age.

Before narrative lock, select and document either a finite, physically staged support installation with food, water, energy, consumables, access and repair budgets covering the interval, or a revision of the personal duration and corresponding age/performance. The latter affects rules, arithmetic, character sheets, dialogue and emotional payoff. The former affects space, props, environmental storytelling and plausibility. Both need a concrete design; neither is a solved claim in 5.7. Small narrative/audio tests can proceed without pretending this is settled.

## Follow-up: personal side missions

The user asked whether more personal missions for other characters are possible. Three **unapproved design candidates**, outside the current campaign contract, are recorded for development:

- **Kian — an unfinished sound recording.** A small act of listening and recording ordinary life could preserve his interests beyond his death. Define the recording opportunity, ownership and playback time before adding it; no new Hoor echo or future information is implied.
- **Nader — a route he can use himself.** A cooperative task could give him meaningful action and decisions with accessible equipment and a usable route. His mobility limitation must not become a rescue spectacle, a cure reward or an explanation of villainy.
- **Yunus — borrowed equipment.** Develop the existing return/keep concept into a personal obligation with a visible owner and a later relational consequence. Essential tools and the player's ending must remain available; it must add a different decision rather than duplicate H04's equipment theft.

These are recorded ideas, not approved scenes, scripted dialogue or production work. Integrating a candidate requires its time/place, player action, alternatives, persistent state and consequence, and a check that it does not repeat an existing beat. Personal missions should be tested for added character understanding rather than counted as content volume.
