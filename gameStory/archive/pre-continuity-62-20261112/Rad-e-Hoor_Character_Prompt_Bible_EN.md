# Rad-e Hoor — Character Prompt Bible

Design package 6.1, character revision A2. English production prompts; Persian source design remains authoritative. Sources: [character owner](Rad-e-Hoor_Character_Bible.md), [screenplay](Rad-e-Hoor_Full_Screenplay_FA.md), [rules](Rad-e-Hoor_Proposal_v5.0.md), [art](Rad-e-Hoor_Art_Direction_Bible.md).

## How to use

Each fenced prompt below is self-contained: copy its entire block. The first block designs a single visual reference; the second requests a 3D model after that identity is approved. Do not send a four-view sheet or a dramatic full scene into a single-subject reconstruction task. Generate separate views using the same approved identity reference; text alone does not lock identity across generations. A generated still is not a model, a generated mesh is not a finished rig, and a rig is not proof of correct Unity behavior.

Human psychological notes direct a pose and performance; do not turn guilt, trust or fear into skin markings, jewelry or background symbols. No new biography, romance, death or mission is authorized by these prompts. Roya/Yunus/Nader dimensions and faces are explicitly new concept proposals. Existing main-cast dimensions are design proportions, not claims about real people. Do not generate exact scene dialogue as texture.

**Sequence:** approve Daniyal and Sara's baseline identities; build Bahram 62 then age the same identity to 82; establish the rest; approve one creature family before scaling its variants. Use a separate first-person hand pass for Daniyal. Nader has an accessibility-specific concept prompt and a held 3D pose decision. Character output has a plain background; use the [layout/background bible](Rad-e-Hoor_Layout_Background_Bible_EN.md) for an environment request.

## CH01 — Daniyal

### Copy-ready visual reference prompt

```text
Create an ORIGINAL character for Rad-e Hoor / The Solar Trace, a first-person audio-survival PC game set in a fictional canyon in Iran's Lut Desert. Use grounded adult proportions and contemporary everyday Iranian workwear. The visual language is stylized 3D volume with selective ink contours, restrained surface-attached hatching and subtle print texture; material color remains readable and anatomy remains functional. Do not imitate a recognizable existing fictional character, actor or branded costume. No photoreal beauty advertising, chibi anatomy, superhero proportions, costume orientalist fantasy, religious symbols or tattoos. Do not add weapons, skull symbols, invented text or plot-signaling magical markings. Neutral material and face presentation must not encode moral worth through appearance.

SUBJECT: CH01, Daniyal. Design proportions: 38 years; 183 cm; 81 kg.
BODY / FACE: A working mechanic and off-road driver, strong forearms, ordinary waist, proportionate thighs, natural shoulders; not a bodybuilder. Warm dark wheat-brown skin with sun exposure on forehead and forearms. Squared jaw, prominent cheekbones, subtly deviated nose, dark hazel eyes with slightly heavy lids. Short wavy black hair, limited gray at temples, five-to-seven-day stubble. A small old stitched scar on the right eyebrow, an old four-centimeter battery burn on the LEFT forearm, callused hands and old knuckle cuts. The right shoulder rests very slightly lower; do not exaggerate it.
CLOTHING / PROPS: Khaki cotton T-shirt, olive work shirt with repaired elbows, thin work vest, camel-colored trousers with a LEFT-knee patch, worn unbranded suede work boots. Baseline is day workwear with NO night jacket or hat on the head; a short brown windbreaker and simple short-brim cloth cap are separate approved variants. A compact frequency meter fastens to the LEFT forearm, chest light on the RIGHT, closed wrench sheath on RIGHT thigh; pen and paper inside the LEFT inner pocket. Hand-touch baseline has bare hands; work gloves are separate loading-only props. No loose metal, towing strap draped across the torso, or tool belt full of invented equipment.
PERFORMANCE INTENT: Precise with a mechanical uncertainty, evasive when a difficult human question arrives. Show a useful hand that can pause before taking over another person's task. A neutral baseline can look relaxed; do not make him permanently ashamed or angry. Later growth is sharing information, asking for a clear signal and accepting help.
STATE AND CONTINUITY: Baseline uninjured; retain old marks. Chapter 5 RIGHT sleeve scratch exists only after shield contact. Chapter 9 RIGHT hand cut and subsequent bandage are separate later variants. Never age him across seven entries.

OUTPUT: one full-body character, FRONT three-quarter view with near-orthographic proportions, entire head/hands/feet in frame, simple warm-gray background, diffuse even studio illumination, clear material boundaries, relaxed neutral A-pose with separated arms and legs, bare visible hands unless clothing above requires otherwise. No cinematic scenery, dramatic cast shadow, text labels, contact sheet, duplicate person, alternate costume in the same image or props obscuring anatomy. Keep all attachment points plausible. This is a single-subject visual reference, not a delivered mesh or proof of rig quality.
```
