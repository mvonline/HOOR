# Rad-e Hoor — Layout and Background Bible

Design package 6.2, environment revision A2. Technical production artifact in English. Sources: [rules](Rad-e-Hoor_Proposal_v5.0.md), [screenplay](Rad-e-Hoor_Full_Screenplay_FA.md), [art direction](Rad-e-Hoor_Art_Direction_Bible.md), [characters](Rad-e-Hoor_Character_Bible.md).

## Scope and use

This bible covers spatial **layout**, visual **background/environment**, and modular **3D asset requests**. It does not mean menu/UI layout. Each location below has two complete copy-ready prompts. Choose a layout prompt to propose a measurable playable space; choose a background prompt for visual direction. A beautiful background cannot replace the layout or validate its rules. Label unsourced dimensions as proposals; use current rules when a generated drawing disagrees.

Exterior L01–L10 are existing named peripheral locations. O01–O03 are optional branches; O04 is a distributed set of existing photo opportunities, not another connected place. ENV-C01–C10 are production views of existing canyon/ending spaces, not ten new story destinations. C05 adjoins C04. L03, L04 and C04 are different places. Build shared architectural/material kits rather than treating every card as a new enormous environment.

## Global spatial contract

The campaign canyon progresses southeast to northwest. Initial road obstruction still leaves the established southeast pedestrian exit. Travel outside uses explicit story-time cuts; do not draw all regional landmarks as real adjacent neighbors. Vehicles park and are recovered along the existing route; companions and tools cannot teleport between frame cuts. Human/vehicle/animal clearance and turning must be tested in blockout.

Three spatial layers must be distinguished: navigation, physical-contact protection, and sound propagation. Sand/stone edges affect movement sound; they are not decorative floor color only. Night enemies follow last heard sources, search 20 seconds within about 1.5 m, then return; continuous sound holds 4–6 m orbit. A plausible drawn wall is not proof of acoustic isolation. Model and measure the actual source, enemy route and retreat, preserving current sound radii.

Time areas require actual thresholds: Bahram's normal-time access versus fast inner room; normal courtyard versus Dara's local near-stasis; Yunus's normal porch versus slow inner cell; the heart's normal perimeter versus arrested center. Do not connect them into one magic tunnel or let player routes require entering a forbidden time zone. F07, the twenty-year supply balance, remains unresolved; no lavish greenhouse illustration closes it.
